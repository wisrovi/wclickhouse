# LTS Support Policy for wclickhouse

This document outlines the Long-Term Support (LTS) policy for `wclickhouse`, ensuring stability, security, and predictability for enterprise deployments.

## LTS Philosophy
A version designated as **LTS** will receive maintenance and security updates for an extended period, focusing on stability rather than new features.

## Support Lifecycle
- **LTS Release Cycle**: Every major version (e.g., 1.x, 2.x) may have an LTS designation.
- **Support Duration**: 24 months of active support + 12 months of security-only support.
- **Patch Policy**: Only critical bug fixes and security patches are backported to LTS versions.

## Versioning
We follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/).
- **Major**: Breaking changes.
- **Minor**: New features (backward compatible).
- **Patch**: Bug fixes and security updates.
